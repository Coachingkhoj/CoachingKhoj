How does CoachingKhoj help students find the best institute?

CoachingKhoj aggregates authentic fee structures, batch sizes, faculty background, and student selection history across 100+ verified JEE and NEET institutes in Noida and Delhi NCR. We offer unbiased comparison, free academic counseling, and instant demo class bookings.

What is the average fee for JEE and NEET coaching in Noida & Delhi NCR?

Top national institutes like Allen, Aakash, and FIITJEE generally charge between ₹1.2 Lakh to ₹2.1 Lakh per year depending on the course. Tech-enabled offline centers like PW Vidyapeeth offer courses ranging between ₹50,000 to ₹85,000 per year.

Are demo classes booked through CoachingKhoj free of charge?

Which locations in Delhi NCR have the best coaching hubs?

Sector 62 and Sector 18 (Noida), Knowledge Park (Greater Noida), Kalu Sarai and South Ext. (South Delhi), Janakpuri (West Delhi), and Laxmi Nagar (East Delhi) are major educational hubs with direct metro connectivity and hostel facilities.

How can students claim fee discounts and scholarships?

Most reputed coaching centers conduct national scholarship entrance tests (such as TALLENTEX, ANTHE, or iACST) that offer up to 90% tuition fee waivers based on performance. You can request scholarship schedules directly on CoachingKhoj.
